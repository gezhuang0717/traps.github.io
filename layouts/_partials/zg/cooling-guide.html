{{ if $zh }}单束散射率 R = (Γ/2)s/[1+s+(2(δ−kv)/Γ)²]。吸收提供 ±ℏk 动量，随机发射提供三维反冲。去除整体漂移后，以速度方差计算各轴温度 Tᵢ=m Var(vᵢ)/kB；平均温度为三轴平均。微运动和有序集体运动仍可进入该诊断，不能自动解释为平衡热温度。{{ else }}Each beam scatters at R = (Γ/2)s/[1+s+(2(δ−kv)/Γ)²]. Absorption gives ±ℏk momentum; spontaneous emission gives random 3D recoil. Axis temperatures use velocity variance after removing centre-of-mass drift, Tᵢ=m Var(vᵢ)/kB; mean temperature averages the axes. Micromotion and collective motion can enter this diagnostic; it is not automatically an equilibrium temperature.{{ end }}

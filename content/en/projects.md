## MASSPASS — Academy Fellowship (PI), 2023–2027
Mass measurements of exotic *N* = *Z* nuclei up to ¹⁰⁰Sn and the vicinity for nuclear physics and nuclear astrophysics, at IGISOL and RIKEN. Research Council of Finland. [Project page](https://www.jyu.fi/en/projects/mass-measurements-of-exotic-nz-nuclei-up-to-100sn-and-the-vicinity-for-nuclear-physics-and-nuclear)

**Team:** Z. Ge (PI); former postdoc Dr. B. Kootte; PhD student M. Winter.

## Low-Q-value decays for neutrino-mass determination
Penning-trap Q-value measurements at JYFLTRAP to find and verify ultra-low-Q β⁻ and electron-capture transitions.

**Team:** Z. Ge (spokesperson); former postdoc Dr. M. de Oliveira Ramalho (theory); PhD student M. Winter.

## MR-TOF mass spectrometry
Design, ion-optical simulation and analysis pipelines for multi-reflection time-of-flight spectrometers.

## RFQ cooler-bunchers and beam preparation
Ion-optical design of radiofrequency quadrupole coolers for cold, bunched radioactive beams.

## Detectors for storage rings
Timing and position-sensitive MCP detectors for the Rare-RI Ring (RIKEN) and concepts for HIAF.

## Research software
Penning-trap particle simulation, MR-TOF calibration and 2D MCP position calibration tools in Python.

## Scientific context of the projects

**MASSPASS.** Masses along the *N* = *Z* line give the two-proton and two-neutron separation energies and the pairing and Wigner contributions to the binding energy, and they test shell evolution towards ¹⁰⁰Sn. For the rp-process they provide the reaction *Q*-values that determine how far the Sn–Sb–Te cycle extends. The nuclei are produced with the IGISOL technique, mass-separated, cooled and bunched in an RFQ and measured with JYFLTRAP and the MR-TOF spectrometer.

**Low-*Q* decays.** The aim is a list of reliable candidates for future direct neutrino-mass experiments: for each transition the decay energy is measured with a precision of a few hundred eV or better, and excited-state energies are checked against nuclear-structure data.

**MR-TOF spectrometry.** Work covers ion-optical simulations (SIMION), the time-of-flight calibration with reference ions (*m*/*q* ∝ (*t* − *t*<sub>0</sub>)<sup>2</sup>), peak-shape fitting and the conversion of frequency or time ratios into atomic masses and mass excesses.

**RFQ cooler-bunchers.** In a radiofrequency quadrupole filled with helium, the RF field confines the ions radially while collisions cool them; a weak axial DC gradient guides them to a potential well from which short, low-emittance bunches are released for trap injection.

**Detectors for storage rings.** In isochronous mass spectrometry the revolution time of a stored ion depends on its mass-to-charge ratio; a ring operated at the transition energy cancels the first-order dependence on the velocity. Thin-foil timing detectors with micro-channel plates measure the revolution times with sub-nanosecond resolution.

**Research software.** Python codes integrate the equations of motion of ions in Penning traps including ion–ion Coulomb interaction, calibrate MR-TOF spectra with reference ions, and correct the position distortions of two-dimensional MCP detectors.
